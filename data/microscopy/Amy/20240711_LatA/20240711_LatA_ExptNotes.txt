Plating:
Prepared 2.5% agarose with L-15 10%FBS 1XABAM.
Slipped a piece of fishing wire 0.23 mm in diameter into the channel and pushed against one edge of channel.
Pre-warmed Ibidi slide on 37C heat block for 2 min.
Flowed in 0.8 e6 cells into channel, let adhere on 37C heatblock for 5 min.
Using 3cc syringe and 18GA sharp needle, flowed in 0.5 ml 2.5% agarose with 10% FBS and 1X ABAM.
Put the slide in the pre-warmed 37C centrifuge with the well that I added the agarose to on the inner part of the swing, closest to rotor.
Centrifuged 37C, 5min, 700xg (9 accel/9 decel).
Immediately put on cool-stir, set to 20C, reading 18.5C for 5 min.
Observed agarose in opposing well, cut out excess agarose from wells with 200ul pipet tip on pipet.
Added 300 ul of L15 10% FBS, 1XABAM and added final concentration of 25uM Latrunculin A to each well.
Imaged.
